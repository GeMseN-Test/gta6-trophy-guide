      Roadmap to the platinum

      One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Story first, stranger strands while they are open, the one possibly missable trophy in Chapter 1, then activities, collectibles and money, and the 100% checklist last. Each stage card shows how many of its trophies you have earned.

      Possibly missable: 1 trophy

      Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation ends with Chapter 1; whether the trophy needs it is unconfirmed. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, and never use cheats on your trophy save.

      At risk in a chapter window: 4 trophies. The other 51 can be earned any time after the story.
